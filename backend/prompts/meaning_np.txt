Explain this medical record entry to the patient in simple Nepali.

Rules:
- Target Grade 8 Nepali. Use everyday words, not literary or Sanskritized phrasing.
- Introduce each medical term in Nepali with English in parentheses.
- Use specific values from the record. Never invent values.
- If information is missing, say so plainly.
- For medicines, explain what they are for, when to take them, and what to watch for.

Entry: Ciprofloxacin 500mg, twice daily, 7 days, for typhoid fever.
